# Practice ASVAB Kiosk — Quick Guide

This is your station's practice ASVAB app. It runs on an iPad, takes about
40 minutes per applicant, and sends you the scores automatically. You don't
need to install anything from an app store, and there's nothing to manage.

## Putting it on the iPad (one time only)

1. Open **Safari** on the iPad (has to be Safari, not Chrome) and go to the
   link you were given for your station.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up, in the
   bottom toolbar).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top right.

That's it — it's now an icon on the home screen, like any other app. Tap it
to open.

## Running a test with an applicant

1. Tap the app icon to open it.
2. Fill in the applicant's last name (required), first name and phone
   number (optional, but the phone number is handy to have on hand if you
   want to follow up with them later), and pick your name from the
   **Recruiter** list.
3. Tap **Start Test**. The next screen says "Hand the device to the
   test-taker now" — once you do, they tap **Begin** themselves, which is
   what actually starts their timer.
4. The app walks them through all four sections on its own — each one is
   timed, and it moves to the next section by itself when time's up or they
   finish early. You don't need to do anything during this part.
5. Between sections, it shows a short break screen with what's coming up
   next and a **Continue** button — the applicant taps that whenever
   they're ready to keep going. No need to get the iPad back from them
   until the whole test is done.
6. After the last section, the screen tells the applicant to return the
   iPad to you. Tap **Continue to Results** to see their score.

## After the test

- The score screen shows their AFQT estimate and a breakdown by section —
  it's fine to review this with the applicant right there.
- There's a **Review next steps** section at the bottom — tap whichever
  option fits and/or type a quick note of your own. There's no save button;
  it saves on its own as you tap, type, or move on.
- The scores and your notes are already sent to your station's results
  sheet in the background — nothing else to do.
- If you (or your chain) review results later, that's done through the
  private dashboard link you were given separately — it's not part of the
  kiosk itself.

## A few things that are normal, not bugs

- **If the iPad sits untouched for 30 minutes after a test**, it resets
  itself back to the start screen on its own. This is intentional — it's
  just clearing the screen for the next applicant, nothing is lost.
- **Swiping back from the edge of the screen doesn't do anything.** That's
  on purpose, so nobody accidentally backs out of a test in progress.
- **If the iPad loses internet mid-test**, nothing breaks — the test keeps
  running locally and the score sends itself once the iPad's back online
  (even if that's not until later).
